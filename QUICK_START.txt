FRAME BUILDER

Extract the complete ZIP to a writable folder, then open Frame Builder.exe.
Keep that filename and folder for stable shortcut targets. You can also retain a custom executable filename.

The app includes all four frame styles from Canvas Frame Builder 3.51.6, reinforced UV-panel joints, the optional Front Snap frame springs, the established stand designs, and STL, STEP and 3MF exports.

Updates
The app checks at startup, every five minutes while online, and when returning to focus after at least two minutes.
Use Download when an update is available. Then use Install and restart after saving your work.
Downloads and restarts require your action.
If a new version fails to open properly, the updater restores and restarts the previous executable.

Settings and updater files are stored in your Windows roaming application-data folder under Canvas Frame Builder.
One previous executable is retained under its updates folder for recovery.
The executable is not code signed. An SHA-256 match verifies download integrity against the published manifest.
